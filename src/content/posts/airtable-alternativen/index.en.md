> **About this comparison:** We are the makers of SeaTable and know the market for no-code databases first-hand. Our focus is on teams in Europe: hosting, GDPR, self-hosting and cost. By these criteria, SeaTable comes out on top. Where other tools are better, we say so in every section.

## In a nutshell

- **Best Airtable alternative for companies and public authorities with GDPR requirements:** SeaTable. Provider based in Mainz, servers in Germany, can also run on your own servers.
- **Best open-source alternative for self-hosting:** Baserow (MIT license) or Grist (Apache 2.0).
- **Best interface for an existing SQL database:** NocoDB.
- **If documents are at the center:** Notion.
- **If you already use Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace:** Microsoft Lists or Google Sheets with AppSheet.

## Why so many teams are looking for an Airtable alternative right now

**Airtable has a new owner.** The software group Bending Spoons completed its acquisition of Airtable on September 4, 2026, at an enterprise value of around 1.3 billion US dollars ([press release](https://investors.bendingspoons.com/newsroom/bending-spoons-completes-acquisition-of-airtable)). Bending Spoons says it will invest heavily in product, support and sales. No specific changes to prices or plans have been announced so far. After earlier acquisitions such as Evernote, however, Bending Spoons cut staff and raised prices ([Reworked](https://www.reworked.co/digital-workplace/bending-spoons-acquires-airtable-for-1285b/)). Many teams are taking this as an opportunity to review their dependence on a single vendor.

**Airtable gets expensive as your team grows.** Airtable Team costs 20 US dollars per user per month with annual billing, Airtable Business 45 US dollars. On the Team plan, a base is limited to 50,000 records, on Business to 125,000. The API is limited too: the Team plan includes 100,000 API calls per month, the Free plan only 1,000. If you connect Airtable to other systems via n8n, Make or Zapier, you will hit this limit quickly ([Airtable plans](https://support.airtable.com/docs/airtable-plans)).

**Airtable is a US provider.** The company is based in San Francisco and is therefore subject to the US CLOUD Act. Data residency in Europe is only available on the top Enterprise Scale plan, and even there metadata such as base names, scripts and user data remain in the US ([Airtable support](https://support.airtable.com/docs/european-data-residency-at-airtable)). We could not find Airtable on the official [EU-US Data Privacy Framework list](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list) (as of September 30, 2026).

**Airtable cannot be self-hosted.** If you need to run your data on your own servers, for example in government, research or healthcare, you need an alternative.

## How we compared

We compared all eight tools based on the vendors' official pricing pages, documentation and legal texts (as of October 5, 2026). As a vendor, we naturally know SeaTable best. Our criteria:

1. **Data model:** links between tables, views, formulas
2. **Limits and price:** records per base, storage, price for a team of 10 users
3. **Provider and hosting:** headquarters, server location, data processing agreement, Data Privacy Framework
4. **Self-hosting:** possible or not, under which license, at what cost
5. **Switching:** import from Airtable, what is transferred and what is not
6. **Automations, scripts and AI**

## 1. SeaTable – the Airtable alternative with servers in Germany

![SeaTable as an Airtable alternative: SeaTable and Airtable compared](seatable-vs-airtable.jpg)

SeaTable is a no-code database from SeaTable GmbH, based in Mainz, Germany. Bases, tables, views and column types work just like in Airtable, so Airtable users find their way around immediately. The cloud runs in data centers in Germany, and if you want, you can run SeaTable on your own servers. Its users include the German Armed Forces (Bundeswehr), Humboldt University of Berlin, the Max Planck Society and the French evaluation agency Hcéres.

**Best for:** companies, universities and public authorities looking for a database like Airtable that need to keep their data in Germany or on their own servers.

**Where SeaTable beats Airtable:**
- Provider based in Germany. SeaTable Cloud runs at Exoscale in Frankfurt am Main and Munich (ISO 27001). A [data processing agreement]({{< relref "pages/legal/avv" >}}) is available for all customers.
- Three deployment options: SeaTable Cloud, a [dedicated system]({{< relref "pages/product/seatable-dedicated" >}}) or [SeaTable Server]({{< relref "pages/product/seatable-server" >}}) on your own infrastructure. Self-hosted, the Enterprise Edition is free for up to 3 users.
- Cheaper: SeaTable Enterprise costs €14 per user per month, Airtable Team 20 US dollars. A base holds 100,000 rows in SeaTable Enterprise, 50,000 in Airtable Team.
- Python and JavaScript directly in the base, without add-ons. Airtable only supports JavaScript.
- A big data backend for large data volumes and a dedicated AI server in Germany for AI automations.

**Where Airtable is better:**
- **AI:** Airtable offers much more AI support. With Omni, you can create apps and agents from a description, and Field Agents fill in fields automatically. So far, SeaTable mainly uses AI in automations and scripts. With the upcoming version 7.0, the AI will also be able to create tables and fields.
- **Column types:** Airtable has 28, SeaTable 26.
- **Support:** Airtable offers optional corporate support with SLAs, SeaTable does not.
- **Ecosystem:** There are more third-party integrations, templates and tutorials for Airtable.

**Who SeaTable is not right for:** teams that mainly want to use AI to build their databases and apps, and companies that need contractually guaranteed response times.

**Switching from Airtable:** When creating a base, choose "Import from Airtable" and enter the base ID and a personal access token. All tables, all rows including attachments, all columns except button, count, lookup and rollup, and the views are transferred. Formula columns are created with the original Airtable formula as a placeholder; you then translate the formula into SeaTable syntax. For more control, there is a [migration script]({{< relref "help/startseite/import-von-daten/migration-von-airtable-bases-zu-seatable" >}}).

**Price:** Free (10,000 rows, 25 users), Plus €7, Enterprise €14 per user per month with annual billing. All details on the [pricing page]({{< relref "pages/prices" >}}).

**Verdict:** The obvious choice if you want to replace Airtable and keep your data in Germany or on your own servers. Learn more on our page [SeaTable as an Airtable alternative]({{< relref "pages/landing-pages/alternatives/airtable-alternative" >}}).

## 2. Baserow – the open-source alternative from the Netherlands

Baserow is a no-code database from Baserow B.V., based in Amsterdam. The core is licensed under MIT and can be self-hosted freely.

**Best for:** teams that want to run and customize an open-source solution themselves.

**Where Baserow beats Airtable:**
- EU provider; according to Baserow, cloud data is stored in Germany ([Baserow](https://baserow.io/blog/baserow-data-residency)).
- Self-hostable, with the core under the MIT license. Premium and Enterprise features are under a separate, non-open-source license ([license](https://gitlab.com/baserow/baserow/-/raw/develop/LICENSE)).
- 3,000 rows for free; Airtable Free offers 1,000 records per base.
- The AI assistant creates tables, fields, views and formulas.

**Where Airtable is better:**
- When importing from Airtable, formulas, lookups, rollups, automations and interfaces are lost ([Baserow docs](https://baserow.io/user-docs/import-airtable-to-baserow)).
- Row limits apply per workspace, not per base.

**Who Baserow is not right for:** teams that want to bring many Airtable formulas and automations with them.

**Price:** Free, Premium $10, Advanced $18 per user per month with annual billing ([pricing](https://baserow.io/pricing)).

**Verdict:** The strongest choice if a true open-source license is a must.

## 3. NocoDB – the interface for existing databases

NocoDB puts an Airtable-like interface on top of a SQL database. The provider is NocoDB Inc., based in the US.

**Best for:** developer teams that want to make an existing Postgres or MySQL database usable for business users.

**Where NocoDB beats Airtable:**
- Self-hosted without limits on records, storage and users.
- 300,000 records on the Business plan, compared to 125,000 on Airtable Business.
- External databases can be connected (from Business).
- The Airtable import also transfers views with filters and sorting ([NocoDB docs](https://nocodb.com/docs/product/bases/import-base-from-airtable)).

**Where Airtable is better:**
- For NocoDB Cloud, neither an EU region nor a data processing agreement is documented ([legal](https://nocodb.com/docs/legal)).
- Formulas are not transferred during import.

**Who NocoDB is not right for:** business departments without technical support. In addition, NocoDB is not under an open-source license in the strict sense, but under the Sustainable Use License.

**Price:** Free, Plus $12, Business $24, Scale $45 per user per month with annual billing. On Plus and Business, you pay for at most 9 users ([pricing](https://nocodb.com/pricing)).

**Verdict:** Strong for technical teams that self-host. For the cloud version, we are missing information on data protection.

## 4. Grist – tables with Python formulas

Grist combines a spreadsheet with a relational database. The provider is Grist Labs Inc., based in New York.

**Best for:** teams that like working with formulas and want to run an open-source solution themselves.

**Where Grist beats Airtable:**
- Grist Core is licensed under Apache 2.0 and can be self-hosted for free.
- Formulas in Python.
- 5,000 records per document for free.
- Affordable: Pro costs $8 per user per month.

**Where Airtable is better:**
- The cloud runs on AWS in the US, without SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification ([Grist docs](https://support.getgrist.com/data-security/)).
- Automations are only available on the Business plan.
- When importing from Airtable, views, interfaces and forms are not transferred.

**Who Grist is not right for:** teams that want to use the cloud version with sensitive data and depend on hosting in the EU.

**Price:** Free, Pro $8, Business $24 (min. 5 users) per user per month with annual billing ([pricing](https://www.getgrist.com/pricing/)).

**Verdict:** A very good choice when self-hosted; in the cloud, less suitable for personal data from the EU.

## 5. SmartSuite – a work platform with many industry templates

SmartSuite is a work platform with a database, project management and many industry templates. The provider is SmartSuite Holdings, Inc., based in California.

**Best for:** teams that want to combine a database with project management and are looking for ready-made templates for their industry.

**Where SmartSuite beats Airtable:**
- Professional costs $32 for 100,000 records, Airtable Business $45 for 125,000.
- Hosting in the EU (Ireland) is possible. The information is contradictory: according to the help center, the region can be selected; according to the pricing page, only on the Enterprise plan.
- Data processing agreement on all plans.

**Where Airtable is better:**
- No free version, only a 14-day trial.
- At least 3 users on the Team plan, 5 on Professional.
- Only 5,000 records per solution on the Team plan.

**Who SmartSuite is not right for:** individuals, small teams on a tight budget and anyone who needs to self-host.

**Price:** Team $15, Professional $32 per user per month with annual billing ([pricing](https://www.smartsuite.com/pricing)).

**Verdict:** Strong on templates and project work, but also a US provider without self-hosting.

## 6. Notion – documents and databases in one

Notion combines documents, wikis and simple databases. The provider is Notion Labs, Inc., based in San Francisco.

**Best for:** teams for whom text and knowledge are central and databases play a supporting role.

**Where Notion beats Airtable:**
- Documents and databases in one tool.
- Cheaper: Business costs €19.50 per user per month, Airtable Business $45.
- Certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

**Where Airtable is better:**
- The database features are weaker, especially for links and large data volumes.
- There is no import from Airtable, only via CSV ([Notion help](https://www.notion.com/help/import-data-into-notion)).
- Data residency in the EU is only available on the Enterprise plan.

**Who Notion is not right for:** teams that manage structured data with many links and automations.

**Price:** Free, Plus €9.50, Business €19.50 per user per month with annual billing ([pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing)).

**Verdict:** An alternative if you have mostly used Airtable as a better wiki.

## 7. Microsoft Lists – the obvious choice with Microsoft 365

Microsoft Lists is part of Microsoft 365 and based on SharePoint.

**Best for:** organizations that already use Microsoft 365 and need simple lists without additional software.

**Where Microsoft Lists beats Airtable:**
- Often already licensed, no additional cost.
- Up to 30 million items per list ([Microsoft](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office365/servicedescriptions/sharepoint-online-service-description/sharepoint-online-limits)).
- Data within Microsoft's EU Data Boundary.

**Where Airtable is better:**
- No import from Airtable, only from Excel or CSV.
- AI features require an additional Copilot license.
- Less flexible with links, views and automations.

**Who Microsoft Lists is not right for:** teams that want to model complex relational databases, and anyone who wants to become independent of US corporations.

**Price:** included in Microsoft 365, from Business Basic at approx. €6.07 per user per month with annual billing (since the price increase in July 2026).

**Verdict:** Pragmatic for simple lists in Microsoft environments, but not a full replacement for Airtable.

## 8. Google Sheets and AppSheet – free and familiar

Google Sheets is a spreadsheet, AppSheet turns it into apps without code. Both are part of Google Workspace.

**Best for:** organizations using Google Workspace and simple use cases on a small budget.

**Where Google Sheets and AppSheet beat Airtable:**
- Included in Google Workspace, from €6.80 per user per month ([pricing](https://workspace.google.com/pricing)).
- Up to 20 million cells per spreadsheet.
- An EU data region from Business Standard.
- Gemini in AppSheet builds tables and columns from a description.

**Where Airtable is better:**
- A spreadsheet is not a relational database.
- Two tools have to be combined.
- No import from Airtable, only via CSV.

**Who Google Sheets is not right for:** teams with linked data, row-level permissions or many users working at the same time.

**Price:** included in Google Workspace, AppSheet on its own from $5 per user per month.

**Verdict:** Sufficient for simple cases, but usually too little database for switching from Airtable.

{{< warning headline="Cost trap: billing per workspace" >}}
With Airtable (Team plan), Baserow, NocoDB and Notion, a subscription always applies to a single workspace. Anyone who works in several workspaces is billed as a user in each of them. If, for example, the same 10 people work in two workspaces, you pay for 20 seats instead of 10.

So plan how many workspaces you need before you buy, and consolidate your bases in one workspace where possible. With Airtable, the double billing only goes away from the Business plan upwards. With SeaTable, every user belongs to exactly one team and is only billed there. Bases can be shared between teams at no extra cost ([Airtable](https://support.airtable.com/docs/billing-overview-and-faqs), [Baserow](https://baserow.io/user-docs/subscriptions-overview), [Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/billing)).
{{< /warning >}}

Two special cases: on Plus and Business, NocoDB charges for at most 9 users; every additional user is free. That is why 10 users cost the same as 9 here. With SmartSuite, you only pay for users who edit data. Anyone who only reads, comments or fills out forms is free.

If you self-host, Baserow, NocoDB and Grist charge no license fee for the basic version, SeaTable for up to 3 users. Server and operating costs come on top.

## Other tools we did not include

Many lists of Airtable alternatives include other tools. We deliberately left them out because they only partially replace Airtable:

- **Teable:** a young open-source alternative (AGPL-3.0) based on PostgreSQL. Interesting for technical teams, but not yet as mature as the tools in our comparison. We are keeping an eye on it.
- **Coda:** combines documents with tables and building blocks for small applications. Like Notion, it is more of a document tool than a database.
- **ClickUp, monday.com, Asana and Smartsheet:** tools for project and task management. They are a good fit if you mainly used Airtable for projects, but not as a general-purpose database.
- **Zoho Creator:** a low-code platform for custom business applications. Powerful, but much closer to software development than Airtable.
- **Microsoft Excel:** not a database, but a spreadsheet. We explain the difference in the FAQ.

## How to switch from Airtable

1. **Take stock of your bases:** Which bases are actively used, by whom, with what data?
2. **Document automations, interfaces and formulas:** None of the tools transfers these completely.
3. **Test import:** Import one base into the new tool and check what is missing.
4. **Set up permissions again:** Permissions are usually not transferred.
5. **Run both in parallel:** Work in both systems for a few weeks before switching Airtable off.
6. **Cancel Airtable and export your data:** Before canceling, also back up all bases as CSV.

## FAQ

{{< faq "Is Airtable GDPR compliant?" >}}
There is no blanket answer. Airtable is a US company and subject to the CLOUD Act. EU data residency is only available on the Enterprise Scale plan, and metadata remains in the US. Whether its use is permissible for your data must be assessed by your data protection officer.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "What changes with the acquisition by Bending Spoons?" >}}
The acquisition was completed on September 4, 2026. No specific changes to prices and plans have been announced so far.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Is there a free Airtable alternative?" >}}
Yes. SeaTable, Baserow, NocoDB, Grist and Notion have free plans. The most generous are SeaTable with 10,000 rows and 25 users and Grist with 5,000 records per document.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Which Airtable alternative is open source?" >}}
Baserow (core under the MIT license) and Grist (Apache 2.0). NocoDB is under the Sustainable Use License, which is not an open-source license in the strict sense. SeaTable is not open source, but can be self-hosted for free.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Which Airtable alternative can be self-hosted?" >}}
SeaTable, Baserow, NocoDB and Grist. Airtable itself, SmartSuite, Notion and Google do not offer a self-hosted version.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Can I import my Airtable bases?" >}}
SeaTable, Baserow, NocoDB, Grist and SmartSuite offer a direct import from Airtable. With all of them, you have to recreate formulas, automations and interfaces.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Is there an Airtable alternative from Google or Microsoft?" >}}
Not a direct one. Google discontinued its product Google Tables and moved it into AppSheet. The closest option is the combination of Google Sheets and AppSheet. At Microsoft, it is Microsoft Lists, and for more demanding applications Power Apps with Dataverse, which requires an additional license. Neither is a relational database like Airtable, but both are worthwhile if you already use Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. Learn more in our comparison [SeaTable as a Microsoft Lists alternative]({{< relref "pages/landing-pages/alternatives/ms-list-alternative" >}}).
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Which Airtable alternative is suitable as a CRM?" >}}
For a simple CRM, any tool with linked tables will do: customers, contacts, quotes and tasks can be connected to each other. SeaTable, Baserow and SmartSuite come with ready-made templates for this. If you need row-level permissions, for example so that each sales rep only sees their own customers, check the "Features compared" table. For email marketing and sales automation at scale, a specialized CRM is the better choice. Learn more in our article [No-code CRM]({{< relref "posts/no-code-crm" >}}).
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "What is the difference between Airtable and Excel?" >}}
Excel is a spreadsheet, Airtable is a relational database with a spreadsheet interface. In Airtable and its alternatives, every column has a fixed type, such as date, single select or file, and tables can be linked to each other. On top of that come forms, views, permissions and automations. Excel is stronger at calculations and analysis, but becomes confusing as soon as several people work with linked data at the same time. Learn more in our article [Databases: 5 advantages over simple tables]({{< relref "posts/vorteile-von-datenbanken" >}}).
{{< /faq >}}
