Eating as cheaply and healthily as possible with little time – a trend from the USA promises exactly that. In stressful everyday life, a **balanced diet** often falls by the wayside. Instead, we often reach for fast food or unhealthy snacks during our lunch breaks, for example, to satisfy our cravings. In this article, you will find out what **meal prep** is all about, what you should keep in mind when meal prepping and how you can create a smart weekly meal prep plan.

## What exactly is meal prep?

Meal prep is short for **meal preparation**. The idea behind it is that you cook your favorite dishes in advance in large quantities so that they are ready to eat over the following days and can be reheated or finished in just a few minutes.

![With meal prep, prepared food is stored in boxes or jars.](ella-olsson-P4jRJYN33wE-unsplash-711x533.jpg)

A variety of dishes can be conjured up from just a few ingredients.

{{< faq "Where does the meal prep trend come from?" >}}

Like many food trends, the hype surrounding meal prepping originated in the USA. There, meal prep was primarily used by fitness enthusiasts, but it quickly found its way into all areas of life.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Who is meal prep suitable for?" >}}

Meal prep is particularly worth trying for people who can't find the time to cook their own meals in their everyday lives. It also helps you stick to dietary resolutions and strengthens self-discipline. Of course, it takes a little effort and enough motivation to try this new way of cooking, but in the end it can make your diet more efficient, healthier and cheaper.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "How many days in advance can you cook meals?" >}}

There are two ways to meal prep. You can either cook **double the amount** so that there is enough food for the next day, or you can go one step further, take the time to **pre-cook the individual ingredients** and then combine them as you like over the following days.
{{< /faq >}}

## Advantages and disadvantages of meal prep

Meal prep has some unbeatable advantages – both over cooking fresh meals every day and over ready meals from the supermarket or take-away restaurant.

### The advantages

- **Saves time:** You don't have to spend hours in the kitchen every evening, but can prepare your meals for several days and combine them in different variations.
- **Saves money:** Ready meals are usually more expensive than cooking yourself, and larger quantities of food cost proportionally less than small packs.
- **Tailored to you:** Unlike ready meals, you can adapt home-cooked dishes to your own needs and preferences and refine them as you like.
- **Promotes healthy eating:** Home-cooked meals often contain less sugar, fat and salt – you know what you are eating and can adapt the recipes to your [nutritional goals]({{< relref "posts/ernaehrungstagebuch" >}}).
- **Reduces food waste:** You can avoid opened packs and spoiled ingredients by buying fresh food in the right quantities and using it up completely right away.

![With meal prep, you only buy the food you really need.](maria-lin-kim-8RaUEd8zD-U-unsplash-scaled-e1700488797696-711x643.jpg)

Targeted shopping saves you money and time because you only buy the food you really need.

### The disadvantages

Sounds too good to be true? Like many food trends, meal prep can't solve every problem, of course. In addition to the obvious advantages, there are also drawbacks that you should consider before you take up meal prep.

- **Limited shelf life:** Some foods and dishes (e.g. salads) are not suitable for long-term storage, as they quickly go bad or lose their texture or flavor.
- **Makes you inflexible:** A spontaneous meal at a restaurant can mess up your meal plan. In the worst case, you have to throw away spoiled food or pre-cooked portions.
- **Monotony:** Even your favorite dishes can lose their appeal when you eat them for the third day in a row. Below, however, you will find out how to add variety to your pre-cooked meals.
- **Storage costs:** Anyone who cooks for several days in advance needs enough space in their fridge or freezer as well as the right storage containers.

![Your meals can become monotonous if you only use a few ingredients.](pexels-keegan-evans-90893-e1700488975339-711x539.jpg)

Be creative and make sure your dishes are as varied as possible.

## Step by step to the perfect meal prep

To make your meal prep mission a success, you first need a **meal plan**. The following five steps will help you with the process.

![How to make your meal prep a success in five simple steps.](Weiss-Minimalistisch-Papier-Notizen-A4-Dokument-e1699963996683-711x868.jpg)

With the right planning, even inexperienced cooks can succeed at meal prep.

### 1\. Plan

First, ask yourself how many days you want to cook in advance. For meal prep beginners, it is a good idea to start by cooking for two or three days to get used to the effort and gradually develop a [routine]({{< relref "posts/habit-tracker" >}}). Look for suitable **meal prep recipes**. These should contain foods that you like to eat. The composition of your meal is also important. For a balanced diet, your meals should contain the following:

- **Proteins:** Good sources are meat, fish and dairy products, but also tofu and legumes.
- **Vegetables:** Fresh and seasonal is best, but frozen vegetables also contain plenty of vitamins and nutrients.
- **Carbohydrates:** Potatoes, rice and pasta are particularly suitable.
- **Fats:** Plant-based fats such as olive oil contain more unsaturated fatty acids and are therefore healthier than animal fats.

### 2\. Shop

The recipes have been selected, now all you need are the **ingredients**. List what you need and in what **quantities** so that you can keep track of everything while shopping. A [shopping list]({{< relref "templates/everyday-life/shopping-list" >}}) also helps you buy only the food you really need for your meals. Make sure to buy fresh and perishable goods only when you can process them right away.

![A shopping list helps you get through your shopping without spending extra money.](torbjorn-helgesen-C4FbCe4L_pw-unsplash-min-scaled-e1700558555269-711x779.jpg)

### 3\. Cook

Now comes the most time-consuming part of meal prep. Take **enough time** to prepare all your food, ideally two to three hours on a day of your choice. Fry, boil, steam or bake the ingredients separately, as they have **different cooking times** and would otherwise turn into a uniform mush. In addition to the main meals, you can also prepare **small snacks** for in between. These could be homemade granola bars, for example.

{{< warning headline="Spices work wonders" text="The right spices add the finishing touch to your dishes. With different spice blends, the same dish tastes different every time and tempts you to explore new worlds of flavor." />}}

### 4\. Store

To ensure that the prepared ingredients stay fresh long enough and retain their flavor, you should **store them separately**. Special [boxes](https://www.chefkoch.de/vergleich/meal-prep-boxen/) with dividers are suitable for meal prep, for example. Store the food in airtight containers made of glass or sturdy plastic. Preferably keep the prepared dishes in the fridge or freeze them.

{{< warning headline="Fill into jars" text="Large jars with screw or clip-top lids are perfect for transporting your food. They are ideal for storing dry foods such as granola or rice, but also for soups or sauces. Most jars are microwave-safe, so you can heat and eat your food right away." />}}

### 5\. Combine

The last step of meal prep is the easiest. Take the prepared components out of the fridge and put together a dish as you like. **Different combinations** provide variety in flavor. Pack your creation securely for transport or heat it up and enjoy your healthy and delicious home-cooked meal right away!

{{< warning headline="It's all in the mix" text="For a balanced diet, you should pay attention to the nutritional values of your food. A composition of 50 to 60 percent carbohydrates, 15 to 25 percent protein and 20 to 30 percent fat is recommended." />}}

## Example of a weekly meal prep plan

An example of a **meal plan** illustrates once again how meal prep works: You have chosen four dishes for a week, all of which contain vegetables, potatoes and/or rice. The **roasted vegetables** form the basis for all the dishes and could, for example, consist of bell peppers, zucchini, carrots, chickpeas, onions and mushrooms.

![Meal prep with roasted vegetables](markus-spiske-jTsGVi6uEZ0-unsplash-min.jpg)

So on **Sunday evening**, you take about two hours to wash and chop the vegetables and roast them in a large baking dish while cooking the potatoes and rice separately. Let the components cool down and fill everything into clean boxes or jars. You can then combine the following dishes from the prepared ingredients:

- **Monday:** Roasted vegetables with potatoes (+ tomato sauce and herbes de Provence)
- **Tuesday:** Chop suey with rice (+ Asian spices such as ginger and soy sauce)
- **Wednesday:** Ratatouille with potatoes (+ broth and nutmeg)
- **Thursday:** Vegetable curry with rice (+ coconut milk and curry powder)
- **Friday:** Vegetable gratin (all leftovers + cheese)

The different spices give the vegetables a different flavor every day. Of course, you can add meat and other ingredients to the dishes to suit your own taste.
