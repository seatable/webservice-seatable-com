      title: 'FAQ – Digital Downtown Management System with SeaTable'
      subtitle: 
      items:
      - q: How does the downtown management system template help with the digitization of citizen participation? 
        a: In SeaTable’s Citizen Cockpit app, citizens can submit concerns, suggestions, and damage reports via a simple web form. The reports are automatically routed to your system and can be assigned directly through automation. With automatic status updates and default assignments, municipalities can use their Citizen Cockpit as a help desk and ticketing system.
      - q: Does the template serve as the basis for an urban data platform? 
        a: Yes, absolutely. Using a REST API, you can centrally collect IoT sensor data and information from other data sources in SeaTable. The Citizen Cockpit visually presents data such as air quality or trash can fill levels. The Citizen Cockpit of the German city of Bad Nauheim serves as a model.
      - q: Can the digital downtown management system be expanded to include custom use cases? 
        a: Yes, you can easily expand the template to include additional tables and app pages, allowing you to design an urban data platform tailored exactly to your needs.
      - q: Is the use of SeaTable GDPR-compliant? 
        a: Yes, SeaTable is fully GDPR-compliant. SeaTable Cloud and SeaTable AI run exclusively on certified servers operated by European service providers in Germany. Access is controlled through two-factor authentication, single sign-on, and role-based permissions. Logs and timestamps provide a traceable record of changes.
      - q: Does SeaTable offer any other templates for the public sector? 
        a: Yes, you’ll find over 100 templates in our template library. We’ve created many of them specifically to meet the needs of government agencies and local governments. The [Municipal Event Management Template]({{< relref "templates/marketing/event-management" >}}) is ideal for coordinating events. You can manage your vehicles using the [Municipal Fleet Management]({{< relref "templates/operations/fleet-management" >}}), and real estate with the [Municipal Property Management]({{< relref "templates/public-sector/municipal-property-management" >}}).
      - q: Can we also host SeaTable ourselves as an urban data platform and digital downtown management system? 
        a: Yes, public agencies can choose between two models—SeaTable Server or SeaTable Dedicated—that allow them to host SeaTable on their own municipal infrastructure.
