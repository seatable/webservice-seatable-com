    - name: "faq"

      title: 'FAQ – Smart City Management System with SeaTable'
      subtitle: 
      items:
      - q: How does the template help digitize citizen participation?
        a: In SeaTable’s citizen dashboard, residents submit concerns, suggestions and damage reports via a simple web form. Reports arrive automatically and can be assigned via automation. With automatic status updates and default responsibilities, municipalities use their citizen dashboard as a help desk and ticketing system.
      - q: Does the template serve as the basis for an urban data platform?
        a: Yes, absolutely. Via REST API, you collect IoT sensor data and information from other data sources centrally in SeaTable. The citizen dashboard visualizes data such as air quality or the fill levels of trash cans. The model for this template is the citizen dashboard of the German city of Bad Nauheim.
      - q: Can the smart city management system be extended with custom use cases?
        a: Yes. You can easily extend the template with additional tables and app pages – for example for parking occupancy, charging stations or event coordination – and thus design an urban data platform tailored exactly to your needs.
      - q: Is using SeaTable for municipalities GDPR-compliant?
        a: Yes, SeaTable is fully GDPR-compliant. SeaTable Cloud and SeaTable AI run exclusively on certified servers of European service providers in Germany. Two-factor authentication, single sign-on and role-based permissions control access. Logs and timestamps document changes traceably.
      - q: Does SeaTable offer other templates for the public sector?
        a: Yes, our template library contains over 100 templates, many of them designed specifically for public authorities and local governments. Coordinate events with the [municipal event management template]({{< relref "templates/marketing/event-management" >}}), manage vehicles with [municipal fleet management]({{< relref "templates/operations/fleet-management" >}}) and real estate with [municipal property management]({{< relref "templates/public-sector/municipal-property-management" >}}).
      - q: Can we host SeaTable as an urban data platform ourselves?
        a: Yes. With SeaTable Server or SeaTable Dedicated, public administrations can choose between two models that allow them to host SeaTable in their own municipal infrastructure – for maximum control over all data in the smart city management system.
      - q: Does SeaTable support e-government requirements such as the German OZG?
        a: Yes. All SeaTable templates have a modular structure and can be flexibly adapted to your municipal requirements. This makes our smart city management system a solid foundation for digitizing your municipality in line with the German Online Access Act (OZG).
