      - q: "Which tool can small companies use to set up an employee database without buying a complete HR software?"
        a: 'There are three options: an Excel or SharePoint list, the personnel module of an HR suite such as Personio, or a flexible database such as SeaTable. SeaTable is a good fit if you want personnel data, departments, and processes such as sick notes or onboarding to be linked and prefer to decide yourself which data you record. With this template, you can get started right away; learn more on our [HR processes]({{< relref "pages/landing-pages/industry-solutions/hr" >}}) page.'
      - q: "Can I use the employee directory template for free?"
        a: 'Yes, you can use the template in SeaTable Cloud with SeaTable Free for up to 25 team members. This also applies to the Sick Leave Management template.'
      - q: "How do I manage sick notes in the employee directory?"
        a: 'There is a dedicated template for this: [Sick Leave Management](https://cloud.seatable.io/copy-template/?template_link_token=66efdfd13de84a9a96a9). Employees report sick via a web form, their managers receive an email at the click of a button, and the days of absence are totaled per person. Official electronic sick notes (for example, the eAU in Germany) are still retrieved via your payroll or HR software; the template serves as an internal overview.'
      - q: "Does the template replace a digital personnel file?"
        a: 'For many small teams, it is sufficient as a central employee database. Features of an HR suite, such as payroll, statutory retention and deletion periods, or self-service for payslips, are not built in. You can store documents such as contracts in a file column; you map deadlines yourself using columns and views.'
      - q: "Where is the personnel data stored?"
        a: 'SeaTable Cloud stores your data in Exoscale data centers in Frankfurt and Munich. You can conclude the data processing agreement (DPA) online. Alternatively, you can run SeaTable on your own servers.'
      - q: "Can I import existing employee lists from Excel?"
        a: 'Yes, you import existing lists into the template via CSV or Excel import and then adjust the columns, departments, and views.'
      - q: "Which templates go well with the employee directory?"
        a: 'For absences, use [Vacation Management]({{< relref "templates/human-resources/holiday-request-management" >}}) and the [Sick Leave Management](https://cloud.seatable.io/copy-template/?template_link_token=66efdfd13de84a9a96a9) template; for working hours, use [time tracking]({{< relref "templates/human-resources/recording-working-time" >}}).
