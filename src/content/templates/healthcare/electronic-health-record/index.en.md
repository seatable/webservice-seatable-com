    - name: "faq"

      title: 'FAQs about the electronic health record template'
      subtitle: 
      items:
      - q: "Is the template free and usable as a full electronic health record?"
        a: 'You can use and customize the template with a free SeaTable account within the limits of your plan. It is a configurable database template, not full-fledged medical software. Connections to national EHR systems or billing are not included. Check before use whether it meets your professional and legal requirements.'
      - q: "Can I use the template on tablets and smartphones?"
        a: 'Yes, the digital patient record can be opened in the browser on tablets and smartphones, and a custom app interface makes mobile use easier. For health data, ensure device security, personal user accounts and task-based access rights. Readability depends on the view setup and screen size.'
      - q: "Can I clearly display medication plans and treatment histories?"
        a: 'Yes, the template includes tables for conditions, treatments and medication intake. Links make the connection between diagnosis and therapy traceable. Documented intake times and doses support professional assessment, but there is no automatic check for side effects, interactions or correct dosage.'
      - q: "Can I visually highlight critical findings, allergies or risk factors?"
        a: 'Yes, add single-select fields and color coding for urgency, treatment status or special notes. Allergies and intolerances are already recorded with type and severity. Agree on binding labeling rules within your team. Color markers do not replace medical assessment and are not an automatic alert system.'
      - q: "Can practices and care facilities use the template in line with GDPR?"
        a: 'GDPR compliance does not result from using a template alone. Clarify the legal basis, data processing agreement, access concept, deletion and retention rules as well as technical and organizational measures in advance. Also consider professional confidentiality and have your specific setup reviewed.'
      - q: "How do I adapt the electronic health record to my specialty?"
        a: 'Add columns, tables and links for the information your specialty requires, such as ICD-10 codes, a medical history form, additional lab values or internal billing codes. These fields are individual extensions and not all part of the template. Additional fields alone do not create a specialized billing function.'
      - q: "Does the template already include a calendar view and Kanban view?"
        a: 'Yes, a calendar view is already included. It clearly displays documented treatment appointments and therapy sessions so your practice team keeps upcoming dates in view. A Kanban view is not preconfigured; add a status field as needed and set up a Kanban view for pending findings and follow-ups.'
      - q: "Which AI functions can be set up for documentation tasks?"
        a: 'Depending on plan and installation, SeaTable offers Classify, OCR, Extract, Summarize and Custom Prompts. Use them to recognize document text, extract data or draft work summaries. These automations must be set up and are not part of the template. Check data protection and results before using real patient data.'
      - q: "Can I connect the electronic health record with other templates?"
        a: 'Yes, you can extend the data structure with additional tables and workflows or integrate content from other templates, such as a medication plan, a to-do list or structured document storage. You configure links, data transfers and permissions yourself; there is no automatic connection to external practice systems.'
