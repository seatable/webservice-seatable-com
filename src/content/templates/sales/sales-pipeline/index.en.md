      items:
      - q: 'What is a sales pipeline and how does it differ from a sales funnel?'
        a: 'A sales pipeline shows which stage each opportunity is in, who is looking after it and what comes next. The sales funnel, by contrast, looks at a group of leads: it shows how many pass through each level and where the largest share drops out.'
      - q: 'What are the stages of a sales pipeline?'
        a: 'Typical stages are lead capture, qualification, proposal and negotiation, followed by closed won or lost. The SeaTable template visualizes sales projects by their status, such as follow-up, negotiation and contract signing. You can add further status values for a finer breakdown.'
      - q: 'Can I use the template as a full CRM for my sales pipeline?'
        a: 'The template is an entry point to a CRM, not a preconfigured complete system. It manages opportunities and owners in two linked tables. For broader customer management, add companies, contacts and activities so that your team can follow the contact history independently of a single deal.'
      - q: 'How does the template help standardize sales processes and forecasts?'
        a: 'Uniform status values, priorities and responsibilities let you manage sales processes by the same rules. For a traceable forecast, define clear criteria per stage and review values regularly. Keep forecasts and signed contract values separate to compare expectations with actual deals.'
      - q: 'Can I adapt the pipeline stages to my own sales process?'
        a: 'Yes, adapt the status values of the sales pipeline to your sales processes. Add “Qualified” or “Proposal sent”, for example, and define when a deal reaches the respective stage. Then check the status grouping in the Kanban board and any existing filters.'
      - q: 'How do executives, managers and the team work with the pipeline?'
        a: 'In a shared pipeline in sales, the team maintains deals and contacts, sales managers check bottlenecks by stage and executives use condensed forecast values for planning. Set up suitable views and reports and define read and edit permissions by task.'
      - q: 'Is SeaTable GDPR-compliant for customer data and deals?'
        a: 'SeaTable Cloud and the integrated AI run on servers in Germany. Whether you use your sales pipeline in compliance with the GDPR also depends on your processes: clarify the legal basis and data processing agreement, and define who may see customer data and when it is deleted.'
      - q: 'How do I calculate the weighted pipeline value?'
        a: 'Multiply the value of each open deal by its win probability and add up the results. A €20,000 deal with a 40 percent probability contributes €8,000. In SeaTable, add two input fields and a formula column for this; the template itself includes a forecast value field.'
      - q: 'How do I build a pipeline in sales with SeaTable?'
        a: 'Import the free template, check the status values and define which criteria a deal must meet to move to the next stage. Then record your open deals, assign owners and use the Kanban board for ongoing coordination in sales.'
      - q: 'Is there support if I have questions about the sales pipeline?'
        a: 'Yes, you can use the SeaTable help section, ask in the community forum or contact support by email. Describe as specifically as possible whether it concerns the template import, the Kanban status values or a forecast formula. This helps narrow down the question faster.'
