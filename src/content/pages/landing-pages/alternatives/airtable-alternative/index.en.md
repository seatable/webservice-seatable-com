      items:
          - q: 'Can I try SeaTable for free?'
            a: 'Yes, of course. The free version of the Airtable alternative SeaTable is ideal for getting a first impression of SeaTable. You can sign up for the free cloud version now and switch to a paid subscription or a self-hosted system at any time later.'

          - q: 'Is Airtable GDPR compliant?'
            a: 'Airtable offers a data processing agreement with EU standard contractual clauses on request. Data residency in Europe (AWS Frankfurt) is only available on the Enterprise Scale plan, and even there metadata such as base names, scripts and user data remain in the US. We could not find Airtable on the [official EU-US Data Privacy Framework list](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list) (as of September 30, 2026). Airtable is also a US company and therefore subject to the US CLOUD Act. Whether its use is permissible for your data must be assessed case by case by your data protection officer. With SeaTable, your contractual partner is SeaTable GmbH in Mainz, the cloud runs in Germany, and you can also run SeaTable on your own servers.'

          - q: 'What changes with the acquisition of Airtable by Bending Spoons?'
            a: 'Bending Spoons completed its acquisition of Airtable on September 4, 2026. Bending Spoons has announced that it will invest heavily in product, support and sales. No specific changes to prices and plans are known so far. If you want to reduce your dependence on Airtable, you can import your bases into SeaTable with a few clicks and test them in parallel.'

          - q: 'What is transferred when importing from Airtable?'
            a: 'All tables, all rows including attachments and all columns except the types button, count, lookup and rollup. SeaTable lists the columns that were not transferred in the new base. Your views are transferred as well. Automations and interfaces are set up again in SeaTable. For more control, there is also a [migration script]({{< relref "help/startseite/import-von-daten/migration-von-airtable-bases-zu-seatable" >}}).'

          - q: 'Can I self-host SeaTable for free?'
            a: 'Yes. SeaTable Enterprise Edition is free for up to 3 users; all you need is a free license file. For more users, you purchase a license. The installation is described in the [Administrator Manual](https://admin.seatable.com/).'

          - q: 'Is Airtable available for self-hosting?'
            a: 'No. Airtable is only available as a cloud service. With SeaTable Server and [SeaTable Dedicated]({{< relref "pages/product/seatable-dedicated" >}}), on the other hand, you can decide today on which infrastructure your data is stored.'

          - q: 'Can someone handle the SeaTable Server installation for me?'
            a: 'We would be happy to install your own SeaTable system. Contact us and we will be glad to send you a quote.'

          - q: 'Can I customize SeaTable to match my corporate design?'
            a: 'In SeaTable Cloud, the options for adapting to your corporate identity are limited – just like with Airtable. With SeaTable Cloud Enterprise, you can customize the logo and colors.<br><br>The situation is quite different with a dedicated or on-premises system. There, you can customize SeaTable extensively: you set the URL, define the templates, roles and permissions available to users, and change the logo and colors. Airtable does not offer you these freedoms.'

          - q: 'Are there special rates for public institutions such as universities, clubs or nonprofit organizations?'
            a: 'Yes. Contact us and we will be happy to tell you more about our discounts.'

          - q: 'How do SeaTable prices differ from Airtable prices?'
            a: 'The entry-level paid plan SeaTable Plus costs €7 per user per month, SeaTable Enterprise €14 (each with annual billing). Airtable Team costs 20 US dollars, Airtable Business 45 US dollars per user per month (as of October 2026). The free plans differ as well: SeaTable Free allows up to 10,000 rows and 25 users, Airtable Free 1,000 records per base and at most 5 editors.'

          - q: 'Does SeaTable offer support?'
            a: 'Yes, our support team is available for all questions about SeaTable, and our community forum offers many answers and tutorials.'
