      items:
          - q: Which tools is SeaTable an alternative to?
            a: SeaTable is an alternative to database and spreadsheet tools such as Airtable, Excel and Google Sheets, to project and list tools such as Asana, Microsoft Lists and Notion, and to specialized software such as Personio for HR and HubSpot for CRM. It is based on a no-code database that works like a spreadsheet.
          - q: What makes SeaTable different from the other tools?
            a: SeaTable combines a relational database, automations, an app builder and AI in one system. SeaTable Cloud runs in data centers in Germany, and with SeaTable Server you can also run SeaTable on your own infrastructure.
          - q: Is SeaTable GDPR-compliant?
            a: Yes. SeaTable Cloud runs with the Swiss provider Exoscale in ISO 27001 certified data centers in Germany. Your data does not leave Europe. If you need maximum control, you can host SeaTable yourself.
          - q: Can I transfer my existing data?
            a: Yes. You can import existing data as an XLSX or CSV file. There is a dedicated import for Airtable bases.
          - q: Is SeaTable free?
            a: 'SeaTable offers a permanently free Free subscription. Paid subscriptions are available for larger teams and advanced features. You can find all details on the [pricing page]({{< relref "pages/prices" >}}).'
          - q: When is SeaTable not the right alternative?
            a: 'SeaTable is not the best choice for every team. Airtable, for example, offers more column types and optional corporate support with contractually guaranteed response times.'
