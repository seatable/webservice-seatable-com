    - name: faq

      title: 'FAQs – SeaTable''s No-Code App Builder'
      items:
          - q: 'Which tool can we use to build internal apps based on our data without developers?'
            a: 'There are three approaches: app builders that sit on top of an existing data source (such as Softr on Airtable, Glide or AppSheet on Google Sheets, Power Apps on Microsoft 365), developer tools such as Retool for existing SQL databases – or a platform that combines database and app, such as SeaTable. SeaTable is a good fit if your data is still in Excel or scattered lists, you want to work without programming and you want data, automations and app in one tool, hosted in Europe or on your own servers.'
          - q: 'How do I turn Excel lists into an app where everyone sees only their own entries?'
            a: 'Import the lists into SeaTable as tables and add a Collaborator column that shows who an entry belongs to. In the App Builder, you create a table page and set a preset filter on this column with the condition “includes current user”. Each person then sees only their own entries in the app. With page permissions (from the Enterprise subscription), you also define who can view and edit which pages.'
          - q: 'Is SeaTable an alternative to Softr or Glide?'
            a: 'Yes, if you want database and app in one tool. Softr and Glide are strong at quickly turning an existing data source into an app, Glide especially for mobile applications. With SeaTable, you don''t need a second data source: tables, links, automations and app live in one platform, with shared permission management.'
          - q: 'Can people without a SeaTable account use the app?'
            a: 'Yes. You can share an app publicly so that anyone with the link can use it without signing in, or create an invitation link with a role and an optional password. No user account is required to use public apps. Anyone who needs to enter or edit data in the app signs in.'
          - q: 'How much does the No-Code App Builder cost?'
            a: 'The App Builder is included in all plans, including SeaTable Free for up to 25 team members. Page permissions for each page and role are available from the Enterprise subscription. You can find current prices on our [pricing page]({{< relref "pages/prices" >}}).'
          - q: 'Can I publish my app in the app store?'
            a: 'No. SeaTable apps are web apps that run in the browser – on smartphones and tablets, too. If your app should appear in the App Store or on Google Play, a tool for native apps is the better choice. Native SeaTable apps for iOS and Android, which let you use your bases on mobile devices, have been announced.'
          - q: 'Where is the app''s data stored?'
            a: 'The app has no data of its own; it shows the data in your base. SeaTable Cloud stores this data in Exoscale data centers in Frankfurt and Munich; you conclude the data processing agreement (DPA) online. Alternatively, you run [SeaTable Server]({{< relref "pages/product/seatable-server" >}}) on your own servers.'
