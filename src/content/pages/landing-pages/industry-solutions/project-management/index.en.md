    - name: faq

      title: FAQ – Project management with SeaTable
      items:
          - q: What projects can I use SeaTable for?
            a: You can use SeaTable for all your projects, such as software projects, [construction projects]({{< relref "posts/bauzeitenplan" >}}), or product development. Thanks to its flexible column types and diverse views – table, Kanban, calendar, timeline, gallery – you can map your project the way your team works.
          - q: Can SeaTable replace our Excel project list?
            a: 'Yes. Import your Excel or CSV file, and everyone works in the same list at the same time – no version chaos and no files sent by email. The list becomes a database: link projects to tasks, clients, or budgets and display the same data as a Kanban board or timeline. Learn more on our page [SeaTable as an Excel alternative]({{< relref "pages/landing-pages/alternatives/excel-alternative" >}}).'
          - q: Are there notifications and reminders?
            a: 'Yes. In the comments on a task, you mention team members with @, and they are notified. With [notification rules]({{< relref "help/startseite/benachrichtigungen/benachrichtigungsregel-erstellen" >}}), SeaTable automatically informs you about new or changed records and sends a reminder when a deadline is approaching. With a filter on the current user, every team member sees their own tasks.'
          - q: Can I share my project plan with external parties?
            a: 'Yes. For read-only access, share a view via an external link – nobody needs an account for that. If clients or partners should only see their own projects or edit data, invite them to an app: with a preset filter on the logged-in user, everyone only sees the records assigned to them. For this, external users need a SeaTable account.'
          - q: Which subscription do I need for efficient project management?
            a: 'That depends on your requirements. The Free plan includes all views, comments, notifications, the app builder, and 100 automations per month and user for up to 25 users. The Plus plan adds private views, sharing of views, permissions for tables, views, and columns, locked rows, and more automations. The Enterprise plan also offers page permissions in apps, big data storage, and even higher limits. You can find details on the [pricing page]({{< relref "pages/prices" >}}).'
          - q: How can I migrate my data to SeaTable?
            a: SeaTable offers numerous import options that make it easy to migrate your data and switch from your previous project management tools. Upload your existing data and your new project management system will be up and running in no time. Thanks to the intuitive, graphical user interface, you'll quickly find your way around.
          - q: Is SeaTable an alternative to Monday, Jira, or Asana?
            a: 'Yes, especially for teams that want to structure their project data freely. All three tools have free plans, but with tight limits (as of October 2026): monday.com Free for 2 seats without timeline, Gantt, and calendar; Asana Personal for 2 users without timeline and Gantt; Jira Free for up to 10 users with board and timeline. SeaTable Free gives up to 25 users all views. You can also run SeaTable on your own server free of charge.'
          - q: When is another project management tool a better fit?
            a: 'If your planning depends on dependencies between tasks and dates should shift automatically, specialized Gantt tools such as Asana, monday.com, or Microsoft Project are stronger. Jira is built for software development with sprints and backlogs. If you are explicitly looking for an open source solution, you will find one in OpenProject, for example. SeaTable plays to its strengths when projects, tasks, clients, and budgets are connected and you want to decide on structure, views, and processes yourself.'
          - q: Can I also use SeaTable to map out my project planning?
            a: Yes, absolutely. SeaTable is suitable as a project planning tool for teams of all sizes. You can plan all steps of your project individually with greater flexibility than with conventional project management tools. Thanks to its numerous features, SeaTable can be used to implement a wide variety of project management methods, such as [Kanban]({{< relref "posts/kanban-methoden" >}}), a [project structure plan]({{< relref "posts/20250317-projektstrukturplan" >}}), or even Scrum.
