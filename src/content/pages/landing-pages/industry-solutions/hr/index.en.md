    - name: faq

      title: FAQ – Manage HR processes with SeaTable
      items:
          - q: 'Is SeaTable really free of charge?'
            a: "Yes. SeaTable Free is free of charge permanently, for up to 25 team members with a total of 10,000 rows and 2 GB of storage. You don't need to provide a credit card."
          
          - q: What does the free version include?
            a: The free version allows you to create digital personnel files, manage HR processes, use time tracking systems, and much more. You also get access to free templates specifically designed for HR to help you get started. If you need advanced features and more storage space, you can upgrade to one of our affordable premium versions at any time.

          - q: "When is SeaTable a better fit than an HR suite like Personio?"
            a: "HR suites like Personio come with payroll, ready-made leave rules, and self-service, and keep them up to date when laws change. SeaTable does not offer this. SeaTable is a good fit if you want to map workflows that standard software does not cover or only at high cost – such as onboarding checklists, equipment handout, applications when you only hire a few people, or training – or if your data should stay on your own servers. Many companies use SeaTable alongside their payroll or HR software."

          - q: "Can employees and managers see only their own data?"
            a: 'Yes. In the [app builder]({{< relref "help/app-builder/app-verwaltung/universelle-app" >}}), you filter a page by a collaborator column with the condition "includes current user". Each user then only sees the records assigned to them – for example their own vacation requests or, via a second column for managers, those of their team. App users cannot change this filter. With roles and page permissions (Enterprise), you also define who sees which page and what may be edited.'

          - q: "How does SeaTable's AI support my daily HR work?"
            a: "With AI automations, you can for example summarize résumés, classify requests, or read receipts via OCR. In the AI chat (SeaTable Cloud and Dedicated), you ask questions about your data, such as open vacation requests or applications – without leaving the platform."
          
          - q: 'How can I integrate and optimize my existing HR processes with SeaTable?'
            a: 'SeaTable is designed to integrate seamlessly with your existing HR processes and systems. Thanks to its flexible API and extensive integration options, you can easily connect SeaTable with other tools and platforms. In addition, SeaTable offers customizable templates and [workflows]({{< relref "pages/landing-pages/industry-solutions/individual" >}}) that allow you to optimize your HR processes such as applicant management, vacation planning, onboarding and offboarding processes, employee development, and much more. With SeaTable, you retain full control over your HR operations and can design your processes in a way that best suits your company.'

          - q: 'How secure is my data in SeaTable, especially with regard to the GDPR?'
            a: 'SeaTable Cloud stores and processes your data in data centers in Frankfurt and Munich, Germany, and you can conclude the data processing agreement online. Alternatively, you run SeaTable on your own servers and keep full control over your data. Table permissions as well as preset filters and page permissions in apps ensure that everyone only sees the data they are allowed to see. You can find details on our [security page]({{< relref "pages/legal/security" >}}).'

          - q: Is SeaTable a good alternative to Personio?
            a: 'Not for the core tasks of an HR suite – payroll, statutory leave accounts, or electronic sick notes. As a flexible complement, or for companies that want to design individual HR workflows themselves, yes: you map applications, onboarding, the employee directory, time tracking, or org charts in SeaTable exactly the way you work. Read more in our article [Personio experiences]({{< relref "posts/personio-erfahrungen" >}}).'
